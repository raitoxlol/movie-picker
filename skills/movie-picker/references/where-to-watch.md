# Legal ways to watch for free

Starting points only — catalogs rotate monthly and services come and go, so always confirm a
specific film with a live lookup (JustWatch lists most of these). Last reviewed 2026-11.

## Free with ads (no subscription)

| Service | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tubi | US, CA, UK, AU, NZ, MX, much of Latin America | Biggest free movie catalog; no account needed |
| Pluto TV | US, UK, much of Europe & Latin America | On-demand plus live movie channels |
| The Roku Channel | US, CA, UK, MX | Works in a browser, not only on Roku devices |
| Plex (free movies) | Most countries | The free ad-supported catalog is separate from Plex Pass |
| Prime Video free-with-ads | Varies | The old Freevee catalog moved into Prime Video; some titles play without Prime |
| YouTube Movies "Free with ads" | US and some others | Look for the "Free with ads" label |

## Free with a library card

| Service | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Kanopy | US, CA, AU, NZ, parts of UK (depends on the library/university) | Strong on classics, arthouse, A24-type titles; monthly play credits |
| Hoopla | US, CA libraries | Borrow-based; limits set by each library |

## Public broadcasters (free in their country)

BBC iPlayer, ITVX, Channel 4 (UK — iPlayer needs a TV licence) · CBC Gem (CA) · SBS On Demand,
ABC iview (AU) · ARD/ZDF Mediathek, ARTE (DE/FR) · RTVE Play (ES) · TVNZ+ (NZ).

## Gone — don't suggest

Crackle and Popcornflix (shut down 2024–25) · Freevee as its own app (folded into Prime Video).

## Not allowed

Piracy sites, torrents, "free movie" mirror sites, and unofficial streams. They're illegal in most
places and often carry malware. The skill sticks to the legal options above.
